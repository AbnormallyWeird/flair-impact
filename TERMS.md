## 1. Description of Service

Flair Impact is a Reddit Developer Platform (Devvit) application that generates statistical insights on post flair engagement within subreddits. It provides interactive engagement visualizations, median comment benchmarks, and customizable flair grouping controls for subreddit moderators and their communities.
